Design for spaces

Work made for a particular wall

Homes, offices, hotels, restaurants, schools and public buildings. Anywhere the space itself — its light, its colours, the way people move through it — should decide what gets painted.

What I need from you
  • Photographs of the wall or room, taken in daylight.
  • Measurements — width and height of the surface, in feet or metres.
  • What the room is for, and who uses it.
  • Your colour scheme, or a photograph of the furniture and flooring.
  • The date it has to be finished by.
