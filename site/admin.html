      How this page works

      Where do my changes go?

      Everything you type is kept in this browser as a draft, so you can close the page and come back. Nothing reaches your website until you press Publish live (when the studio server is running) or export the file and upload it into your site's data/ folder — artworks.js for paintings, products.js for materials.

      Do I have to resize my photographs?

      No. Drop the photograph straight from your phone or camera — this page shrinks it to 1800 pixels on its longest side, which is plenty for the web and keeps your site fast.

      How does someone pay for materials?

      They don't, on the website. An order arrives as an itemised email; you reply with the total including packing and postage to their pin code, and a UPI or bank transfer request. That keeps the site free to run and keeps you in control of postage — which for jars of pigment is never a flat rate.

      Is this page private?

      Not by itself. On plain web hosting anyone who guesses the address can open it, though they can only change their own copy — your live site can only be changed by uploading a file, which needs your hosting password. If you want the panel properly locked, run the optional studio server, which asks for a password before it will publish anything.
