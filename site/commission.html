Commissions

Have a painting made for you

A portrait from your photographs. The landscape of a place you return to. A large work sized to a particular wall. A study of your city's river, your family's village, your state's festival dress. Tell me what you have in mind — the form below takes about three minutes and gives me everything I need to quote honestly.

I take a limited number of commissions each year so that each one gets proper time. If I cannot fit yours in, I will say so straight away rather than keep you waiting.

Painting a wall directly, or planning a set of works for a whole room, office or hotel? That is design work for spaces — the brief there asks about the room instead of the painting.

What you get
  • A written quote with size, medium and a completion date before anything is charged.
  • A sketch or colour study for your approval before the painting begins.
  • Progress photographs at agreed stages.
  • A signed original with a dated certificate of authenticity.
  • Insured packing and delivery, quoted at cost.
